# About JAV

What JAV values, gathered from partner posts and press. JAV has no website of its own, and its Facebook pages weren't readable. Confirm everything here with JAV.

## Values

- **Atonement:** founder Joshua Blahyi frames JAV as repaying the harm he did in the war. "The country we destroyed is the country we want to rebuild." "These kids are victims, not criminals."
- **Christian faith:** an evangelical ministry. Blahyi shares his testimony and asks participants to "offer their lives to Christ and turn away from drugs."
- **Trades and self-sufficiency:** carpentry, plumbing, painting, bricklaying, construction, farming, chickens.
- **Dignity and a fresh start:** "stability, belonging, and a fresh start" (Hope for Liberia chair appeal).
- **Transformation stories:** graduates who became evangelists, family men or students. Claims 700–1,000 trained since 2007 and an 84% success rate.
- **Funding and partners:** unfinished buildings, a $500k appeal for facilities and farming, food donations from the KYB and Joseph Nyuma Boakai foundations.

## Facts

- Rehab centre in Mt. Barclay, Montserrado. The training complex is ~25 km north of Monrovia.
- Fees up to $1,700 for 6 months, or $3,740 for the full 18 months.
- Early coverage is about former child soldiers. Recent coverage is mostly about young men addicted to drugs.
- Main partner: Hope for Liberia Canada, now **hope360.ca** (registered Canadian charity). Its homepage doesn't mention JAV. Its planned "Hope Village" rehab centre appears to be linked to JAV.
- No logo found.

## Watch out for

- **Criticism:** a New Narratives investigation reported new patients chained in during withdrawal, punishments for escaping and no evidence for the success rate. Many Liberians also doubt Blahyi. Visitors will find this, so avoid outcome claims the site can't back up.
- **Audience:** decide whether to lead with former child soldiers, addiction or both.
