# Accepting Western Donations

## Options

| Route | Tax-deductible | Effort | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **GlobalGiving** | US/UK | Low–medium | Vetting required. ~5–15% fee. Pays out to a Liberian bank. |
| **Fiscal sponsor** (US 501(c)(3)) | US | Medium | Receives donations for JAV and grants them on. Prefer one active in West Africa. |
| **CAF America** | US | Medium–high | Suits large gifts and grants, not small online donations. |
| **"Friends of JAV" US nonprofit** | US | High | Needs a US board, an IRS application and annual filings. The board must control grants. |
| **Bank wire** (USD account, e.g. Ecobank, UBA) | No | Low | High fees. Suits large or diaspora donors. |
| **Mobile money** (Orange Money, MTN MoMo via Remitly, WorldRemit, Sendwave) | No | Low | Cheap, but awkward for donors. |
| **Western intermediary** (GoFundMe, Donorbox) | No | Low | Depends on one person. Stopgap only. |

Stripe, PayPal, Donorbox and Givebutter likely can't pay out to Liberia directly. Confirm before relying on them.

## Recommendation

1. Apply to GlobalGiving or a fiscal sponsor.
2. List bank wire and mobile money details for direct donors.
3. Revisit "Friends of JAV" once donations are regular.

## Prerequisites

- Current Liberian registration and NGO accreditation
- Basic financial records

## Site

Make `/donate` a static page that links to the partner's hosted checkout, with bank and mobile money details kept in Sanity. No server routes.
